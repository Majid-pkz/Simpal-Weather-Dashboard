How do we pick a laundry window?

We compare future forecast points between 8 am and 6 pm in the city's local time, using daylight points where available. A candidate window needs at least two consecutive points, three hours apart, with precipitation probability at or below 20%, no forecast rain or snow, temperature above 5°C, wind below 30 km/h, and gusts below 45 km/h when reported.

Lower humidity, warmth and a gentle breeze improve the score. These are simple planning rules, not a scientific drying model. Windows span the first to last qualifying point; weather between points can change. “Peak rain chance” is the highest precipitation probability at a displayed point, not the probability for the entire day. Days may have partial coverage.

Forecasts cover about five days, which can touch six calendar dates. Times use OpenWeather’s city UTC offset; a daylight-saving transition within the forecast may shift them by an hour. Fabric, shade and airflow affect drying. Recheck before hanging washing outside.
